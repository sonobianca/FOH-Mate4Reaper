# FOH-Mate
Live sound tools for REAPER, made by a live sound engineer.

## Plugins Host, the first module
A rack-based FX panel built with ReaImGui. A few REAPER tracks become racks, each with its own input and output routing, a chain of FX, a level, mute and bypass, and input and output meters. All the racks live in one window, away from your playback tracks, so the mixer and the track view stay clean.

## What it does
- **Racks are tracks.** Any track named "RACK: ..." becomes a rack, hidden from the track list and kept off the master.
- **Edit and Show modes.** A cue-light switch locks the racks and their FX while you perform. Level, mute, bypass and FX on/off keep working.
- **Quick FX handling.** Add, insert, replace, delete, reorder, copy and move FX with menus and drag and drop.
- **Sidechains without the routing maze.** A key button on every FX that has a sidechain input.
- **Mono, stereo and mono-to-stereo racks.** FX switch to their mono or stereo version on their own.
- **Level match,** duplicate a rack or use it as a template, rack colors and a Daylight theme.
- **Latency watchdog.** Racks and FX that add more latency than your limit flash an alert. A plugin installed in several formats (AU, VST3...) can count at its highest latency, since some formats report 0.
- **Plugin list** of every installed FX, with favorites, developers, latency and scan status.

## Requirements
REAPER 7.x and ReaImGui (install it from ReaPack, ReaTeam Extensions). Developed and tested on macOS with REAPER 7.84; Windows and Linux are untested.
